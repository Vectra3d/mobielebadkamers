# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Dutch homeowners (often 35–70) who are about to renovate their bathroom, or who suddenly can't use it after a leak. They usually find the site via Google on a phone, in the evening at the kitchen table, slightly stressed and comparing options. Secondary: contractors (aannemers) arranging a temporary facility for a client, and people with temporary reduced mobility who need a ground-floor shower.

Job to be done: "Find out, within a minute, whether I can keep showering at home during the work, what it costs, whether it fits on my driveway or through my door, and how to book it."

## Product Purpose
MobieleBadkamer.info (part of Nonstop Rioolreiniging, Idskenhuizen, Friesland) rents out two products:
- **Mobiele badkamer**: a heated trailer unit with shower, toilet, washbasin with mirror and socket. €137,50 per week excl. btw. Delivered, connected and collected, or self-towed with any car with a tow bar. Minimum rental 14 days.
- **Mobiele douchecabine**: a compact indoor shower cabin (78 cm wide, 200 cm high) with built-in boiler and drain pump. €129 first week, €99 per following week, incl. btw.

Success = a visitor understands which product fits, sees the price, and sends a rental request or calls.

## Brand Personality
Practical, reassuring, local. Like a good Dutch installer: says what it costs, shows the real thing, turns up on time, no fuss. Calm confidence rather than salesy urgency. Plain Dutch with "je", short sentences, a dry touch of humour is fine ("niet langer aankloppen bij de buren").

## Anti-references
- Stock luxury-bathroom photography that isn't the product (the current hero does this).
- SEO-stuffed copy that repeats "badkamer wagen huren" in every sentence.
- Generic WordPress-theme blue gradients over a photo, tiny white body text on images.
- Glossy SaaS landing-page tropes: hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, eyebrow labels on every section.
- Emergency-plumber shoutiness (flashing red, "BEL NU!!").

## Design Principles
1. **Answer the three questions above the fold**: what is it, what does it cost, how do I get it.
2. **Show the real unit.** Real photos and real dimensions beat adjectives.
3. **Help people choose.** Badkamer vs douchecabine is the main decision; make the difference obvious.
4. **One clear next step everywhere**: request availability, or call. Phone is first-class on mobile.
5. **Say it once.** Each fact (price, specs, FAQ) lives in one place and is linked, not repeated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Audience skews older: body text ≥17px, high contrast, generous tap targets (≥44px), real labels on every form field, no information conveyed by colour alone, reduced-motion respected, works without JavaScript for reading content.
