## The request form
The form picks the best way to send, automatically:

1. **Your own web host (PHP): `send.php`.** Upload it to the web root next to `index.html`. It checks every field again on the server, blocks other websites and bots (hidden honeypot field, max 5 requests per hour per visitor), recalculates the price, and emails the request to info@mobielebadkamer.info. Pressing "Reply" answers the customer. Settings are at the top of the file: `MAIL_FROM` must be an address on your own domain, or your host may drop or spam-flag the mail. Send yourself one test request after uploading.
2. **No PHP (GitHub Pages), or `send.php` fails: FormSubmit** (formsubmit.co, free, no account). The first request triggers a one-time "Activate Form" email to info@mobielebadkamer.info. Click the button in it; requests sent before activation are not delivered. FormSubmit then also sends a random alias: put it in place of the address in `FORMSUBMIT_ENDPOINT` (top of `assets/js/site.js`) and in the form `action` in `contact/index.html` to keep the address away from spam bots.
3. **Both fail:** the visitor sees an error with a ready-made email link and the phone number.

Privacy: with FormSubmit, request details pass through a third party. With `send.php` they go straight from your server to your mailbox. Mention the form in your privacy statement either way.

## Editing
Pages are generated by `_source/build.py` (shared header, footer, FAQ and prices live there once). Edit and run `python3 _source/build.py` (needs Pillow), or edit the HTML files directly.
